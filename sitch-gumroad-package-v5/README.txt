What to upload to Gumroad
-------------------------
Upload ONLY the PDF: sitch-access-guide.pdf

Gumroad will deliver this PDF to every buyer automatically. The PDF tells buyers to visit
play.sitchthegame.com and enter their license key.

The PDF does NOT contain the actual license key. Buyers receive their unique license key from
Gumroad on their receipt.

How to set up Gumroad license keys
----------------------------------
1. Open your Gumroad product page (Sitch — Family Edition).
2. Go to the product settings.
3. Turn ON "Generate a unique license key per sale".
4. In the "Content" tab, add a message such as:
   "Your license key is on your receipt. Use it at play.sitchthegame.com."
5. Save the product.

Now each buyer will receive a unique license key like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX after purchase.
